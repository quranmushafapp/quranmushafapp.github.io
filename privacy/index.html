Privacy policy

Quran Mushaf (Android)

Effective 19 November 2026

Quran Mushaf is a mushaf for reading the Qur'an, with help for memorising it. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We (the app's developer) do not collect, receive or sell any information about you.

What stays on your phone

Everything you make in the app is kept only on your phone: your recordings, hifz plan, reading plans and khatms, progress and practice times, slips, weak ayahs, highlights, bookmarks, the name you put on a shared card, and your settings. We cannot see any of it.

Microphone

The microphone is used only while you record, or during your turns in listen and repeat. Recordings are saved on your phone and nowhere else.

What the app downloads

The app connects to the internet only to fetch Qur'an content:

  • Recitation audio, streamed from everyayah.com and mp3quran.net when you listen, and kept on the phone to play again.
  • The Madani mushaf pages, downloaded from GitHub (github.com) if you choose that mushaf.

Like any website, these servers see your phone's IP address and which file is being fetched. The app sends them nothing about you. Their own privacy policies apply to what they log.

Sharing

Recordings, videos and cards leave your phone only when you tap Share, and go only to the app or person you choose.

Backups

A backup is a file the app makes when you ask, saved where you choose (on your phone, or a cloud drive you pick). It contains your recordings, plans and progress; whoever has the file can restore it, so keep it somewhere safe.

If your phone's own backup (Android backup to your Google account) is switched on, Android may include the app's data in it. That is between you and Google, under Google's privacy policy, and can be switched off in your phone's settings.

Reminders

Reminders are made on your phone, at the times you choose, and only if you switch them on. The app restarts them after your phone restarts.

Purchases

Quran Mushaf Plus is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your card or payment details, only Google's confirmation that Plus is unlocked.

Children

The app is for readers of every age. It collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Deleting your data

You can delete any recording in the app. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in your phone's settings, removes everything it kept. Backup files you saved are yours to delete.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and online, with a new date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at github.com/quranmushafapp/quran-mushaf-data/issues.
